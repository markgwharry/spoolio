# StatusChip

One short phrase of status for a spool or printer, always in words.

- `tone`: `shelf` for good states (ledger-blue), `loan` for on loan or in progress (sepia), `warn` for attention (oxblood), `neutral` otherwise.
- `dot` adds a small dot for live states such as a running print.
- Never rely on the colour alone; the words carry the meaning.
