# Sticker

A round library sticker marking a spool that needs attention: **L** (oxblood) for low stock, **D** (ink) for due for drying.

- Provide `kind`. It carries its own accessible name.
- Used at the top of a `SpoolSpine` in place of the accession number, and in the legend beside the shelves.
- Show a legend wherever stickers appear, so the letters are explained.
