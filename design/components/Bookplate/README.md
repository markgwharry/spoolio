# Bookplate

The printed spool label, black on white at 640 × 360 for 62 mm label stock, with an "Ex libris" line, the accession number, the colour name, printing facts and a QR code.

- Provide `no`, `name`, `subtitle`, up to three short `specs`, `acquired`, and `qr` (the rendered code; without it a labelled placeholder shows).
- It always prints in `print-ink` on `print-paper`, whatever the theme.
- The QR code opens the spool's record, which is how a spool is lent from a phone.
